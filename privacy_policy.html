FileForge

Privacy Policy

Short version: FileForge does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your device, under your control.

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how FileForge ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the application. FileForge is a desktop application for Windows. It does not connect to any remote servers, does not create user accounts, and does not transmit any information over the internet.

Effective date: 21 May 2026

2. Data We Do Not Collect

FileForge does not collect, transmit, or share:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, IP address)
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports sent to a remote server
  • Payment information
  • The content of any files you open or process
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

FileForge stores the following data on your device only, in the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\FileForge\:

  • Settings (settings.json) — your preferences such as theme, default output folder, and entity configuration. This file never leaves your device.
  • Audit trail (audit.db) — a local SQLite log of file operations performed within the app, including timestamps, input/output paths, and the operator name you optionally enter in Settings. This log is stored solely on your device and is never transmitted externally.
  • Payment entity configuration (config\entities.json) — sample entity definitions you customise for your organisation. Stored locally only.

You can delete any or all of this data at any time by removing the %LOCALAPPDATA%\FileForge\ folder.

4. Files You Process

FileForge reads and writes files on your local file system as directed by you. The content of those files is never read by us, never sent to any server, and never retained by the app beyond your active session. The app has no network access and cannot upload file contents anywhere.

5. Internet Connectivity

FileForge does not require an internet connection and does not make any outbound network requests during normal operation. No data is sent to or received from any remote server.

6. Third-Party Services

FileForge does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing service. The purchase of the app is handled by the Microsoft Store or other authorised distribution platforms, each of which has its own privacy policy governing the transaction.

7. Children's Privacy

FileForge is a business productivity tool not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version at this URL with an updated effective date. Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly, so we encourage you to review this page periodically.
