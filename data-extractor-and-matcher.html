Data Extractor and Matcher

Privacy Policy · by FileForge

Short version: The app processes your files entirely on your own device by default. The only data that ever leaves your device is what you explicitly send to an optional third-party AI service you've configured yourself with your own API key.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This policy covers the Data Extractor and Matcher Windows desktop app ("the app"), published on the Microsoft Store by FileForge.

What the app does with your files

You point the app at a folder of scans (PDF/image/text) and an Excel file. The app:

  • Reads the scans and extracts fields (e.g. name, IBAN, email, invoice reference) using local OCR (Tesseract) and pattern matching.
  • Fuzzy-matches extracted names/values against rows in the Excel file you provide.
  • Writes results back to a new Excel copy plus review_*.csv and audit_*.csv files, saved to a folder you choose (by default, Documents\Data Extractor and Matcher on your own machine).

None of this — scans, Excel contents, extracted fields, or results — is uploaded anywhere, unless you opt in to a cloud extractor as described below.

Optional cloud extractors (opt-in, your own keys)

The app supports two optional cloud-based extraction modes, selectable in settings. Both are off unless you configure them:

  • Azure AI Document Intelligence — if you supply your own Azure endpoint and API key, scanned documents are sent to your Azure resource for extraction.
  • OpenAI (LLM extractor) — if you supply your own OpenAI API key, scanned document text is sent to OpenAI's API for extraction.

In both cases, the data leaves your device only because you provided credentials for a service you control, and only for the documents you choose to process in that mode. The app does not embed, share, or use any API keys other than the ones you enter. Review Microsoft's, Azure's, and OpenAI's own privacy terms for how they handle data sent to their services.

Microsoft Store license check

On launch, the app asks the Windows Store service (StoreContext) to confirm your copy is licensed. This is a standard Windows API call handled by the operating system to verify your purchase; the app does not see or store any payment or account information itself — that is handled entirely by Microsoft.

What the app does NOT do

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No sale or sharing of your data with third parties for marketing
  • No network access at all in local/default mode

Data retention

All output files (Excel copies, review/audit CSVs) are written to a folder on your own device that you choose, and remain there under your control until you delete them. The app itself does not retain copies elsewhere.

Children's privacy

The app is a general business/productivity utility not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
